Configure a new game

Open New game and assign each player a one-letter code, name, colour name and colour. Saving creates four riders per player. For example, code B maps B1 through B4 to that saved player and colour.

Start with your hand

Choose your team at the top, then open Card knowledge. Enter the exact movement cards for your four riders as comma-separated values. The Race screen immediately turns them into legal move choices and shows each destination.

After an email adjudication

Paste the new position block and replacement block into Update round. A position change is treated as the card played. The replacement is added to that rider’s known hand. For opponents, the tracker keeps known cards separate from unrevealed slots.

Accepted format

Positions use 36: R1, B2. Replacements use R1, B2: 7. Blank lines and extra spaces are fine. Rider IDs are a saved team code followed by 1–4.

A note about inference

A played movement becomes public because it equals the change in position. If it matches a known card, that card is removed from the active hand; otherwise, one hidden slot is consumed. Either way, the inferred card remains visible in gray with a strikethrough until the next round. Published replacements are added as active known cards.
